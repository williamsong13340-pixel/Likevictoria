Concept art for the main screen of a premium PC grand-strategy economic simulation game set in 1962 (a postwar world-economy game; there are no military units anywhere). Widescreen composition.

Center: a gorgeous world map in a refined modern-atlas style: soft shaded-relief mountains, muted hypsometric land tints, layered deep ocean blues with subtle bathymetry, crisp thin coastlines and borders, countries washed with translucent pastel political colors, elegant curved serif country labels, small city dots.

UI: elegant and minimal, semi-transparent ivory panels with thin warm-gray dividers. Top bar: the date "1962年3月", game speed controls, and compact indicators (GDP growth, inflation, unemployment, treasury), each with a tiny sparkline. Right panel: an economy dashboard with a clean line chart, a population pyramid, and a small table of commodity prices with flat monochrome icons (wheat, oil barrel, steel beam, car, microchip). Bottom-left: a minimap and map-mode buttons.

Palette: ivory, slate blue, muted teal, warm gray, small accents of burnt orange. Typography: modern, clean, highly legible. Mood: calm, intelligent, luxurious. Ultra-detailed, crisp, professional game UI design.
